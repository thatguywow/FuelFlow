## Short description (80 characters max)

> Calorie and macro tracking that measures your metabolism. No account, no cloud.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

> FuelFlow is a calorie and macro tracker that works out your actual energy
> expenditure from your own logging, instead of guessing it from a formula.
>
> **It measures, rather than assumes.** Most trackers hand you a number from a
> textbook equation and never revisit it. FuelFlow watches what you eat and what
> you weigh, and after about two weeks it can tell you what your metabolism is
> really doing — with an honest uncertainty band, so you can see how much to
> trust it. Every target in the app is built on that number, and you can inspect
> it rather than take it on faith.
>
> **Everything stays on your phone.** No account. No sign-up. No server of ours
> holding your diary. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it
> to. Export an encrypted backup whenever you want.
>
> **It works without a signal.** The food database lives on your device, so
> searching and logging work on a plane, in a basement, or abroad with data
> switched off.
>
> **Scan what you eat.** Barcodes resolve against a database of packaged
> products. Nutrition labels are read by the camera, on the device — useful for
> market produce and home cooking, which have no barcode at all.
>
> **Free, and free software.** No subscription, no premium tier, no adverts, no
> feature held back. Licensed under the GPL, developed in the open, and yours to
> read, fork or audit line by line.
>
> Track calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre and thirty-odd micronutrients. Build
> recipes from ingredients. Log water, exercise and weight. See your trends over
> weeks rather than days.

## Ahead of the first upload

1. Create the signing key and add the four secrets — see the comment at the top
   of `.github/workflows/android-release.yml`.
2. Run that workflow with a version name and code.
3. Upload the `.aab` from the run's artifacts to a **closed test** track first.
   Play now requires a period of closed testing before a personal developer
   account can go to production.
4. Fill in this listing, the data safety form and the content rating.
